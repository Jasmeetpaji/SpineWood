##SPINEWOOD THE GAME

This game is based on the medieval villages and their old delivery system.
this game gives an very cozy vibe while playing.
the game contains 2 villages and a market and some other structures

##GAME MECHANICS:

Collect supplies from the market and deliver them to the destinated village
sleep in your tent to skip 8 hours
gain the trust of the villagers by delivering them supplies 
This Game also has a reputation system

##GAME FEATURES:
Game contains interaction with the villagers through delivering them items collected by the player from the village market 
day and night cycle with really cool skies 
delivery system for the villagers
trust bar which increases with per delivery

##HOW TO PLAY:

After spawning walk to the market and collect supplies from the stalls in the market and then look at the delivery bar which will tell you where to deliver the supplies
deliver the supplies and then gain the trust of the villagers 
walk to the tent if you wanna sleep and skip 8 hours of the day
